# ScrollSprint Creative — Process

Source: `components/scrollsprint-site.tsx` (approach section, `#approach`) on https://scrollsprint.online/

## Definition

A focused sprint gives a paid-social team more angles to test with one product.

## Steps

1. Review the product page, current ads, assets and goals.
2. Identify buying moments, hooks, product demonstrations and story directions.
3. Produce finished vertical ads and variations, with captions and sound where included. One bounded revision round refines the batch.
4. The client launches the ads and uses the results to inform the next sprint.
