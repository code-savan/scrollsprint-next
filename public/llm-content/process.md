# ScrollSprint Creative — Process

Source: `components/scrollsprint-site.tsx` (process section, `#process`) on https://scrollsprint.online/

## Definition

A creative pipeline, not one expensive commercial. The goal is to give a paid-social team multiple credible creative directions to test quickly — not to make one video and hope.

## Steps

1. Send the product: share your URL, existing ads and what your team already knows.
2. Map the angles: identify the strongest hooks, pain points and concepts.
3. Build the sprint: production, editing, voiceover, captions and variations.
4. Test + learn: your team tests. The learnings shape the next creative batch.
