# ScrollSprint Creative — Services

Source: `components/scrollsprint-site.tsx` (services section, `#services`) on https://scrollsprint.online/

## Definition

ScrollSprint Creative turns one product into multiple hooks, angles and finished video creatives so a brand's team has more credible creative to put into market.

## Services

- Product demos: show the mechanism, use-case and payoff without burying the product.
- UGC-style concepts: creator-native structures without turning the brand into generic talking-head content.
- Hook variations: one core idea becomes multiple openings so the test actually tests the hook.
- Testing velocity: finished variations, captions, sound and CTAs ready for the paid-social queue.

## Format scope

- The primary format is vertical paid-social creative for Meta, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- Other formats can be included by scope.
- The core offer is creative production. Media buying is separate unless explicitly included in a custom engagement.
