# ScrollSprint Creative — Overview

Source: `app/layout.tsx` (site metadata) and `components/scrollsprint-site.tsx` (hero) on https://scrollsprint.online/

- ScrollSprint Creative is a direct-response ecommerce creative service.
- Core line: "More ads to test. Less production drag."
- What it does: product-first video creatives for ecommerce teams that need fresh hooks, new angles and testing variations — without waiting weeks for production.
- Positioning: "Direct-response creative for ecommerce" and "Your media buyer cannot test ideas you never produce."
- Primary format: vertical paid-social creative for Meta, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- AI is production infrastructure, not the headline offer.
