# ScrollSprint Creative — Overview

Source: `app/layout.tsx` (site metadata) and `components/scrollsprint-site.tsx` (hero) on https://scrollsprint.online/

- ScrollSprint Creative makes short-form ad creative for ecommerce brands already testing or running paid traffic.
- Core line: "More ads to test. Less production drag."
- What it does: strategy-led creative with stronger hooks, clearer product demonstrations and more directions to test.
- Primary conversation: send a product page or current ad for a focused recommendation before choosing a one-time creative sprint.
- Primary format: vertical paid-social creative for Meta, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- AI is not the headline offer. Creative testing is.
